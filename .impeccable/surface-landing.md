# Surface brief — Landing page (`/`)

## Scope and visitor mode

Persuade. Visitor: Urdu/Hindi poetry lovers and Sufi seekers carrying an emotion (dard, ishq, hijr, junoon) and looking for words for it. They arrive late at night, often on a phone, and must believe within one screen that this place understands the *meaning* behind emotions — not just poetry words.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Job: feel understood, then generate a sher. Action: the category's action in working form — ENTER MEHFIL (into the studio), with US DARD KO LIKHO as the signature secondary. Proof/demonstration: a live demo sher generated in the page from a real emotion combination (labeled demo when running without credentials). Constraints: brief-pinned palette (deep charcoal, midnight black, warm ivory, antique gold, burgundy, muted bronze); Urdu headline verbatim; no lyrics ever shown; engine separation is a product truth, shown as three niches.
